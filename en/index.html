About

Prometis was born from a simple observation: scams evolve faster than our reflexes. Fake advisers, fake texts, voices imitated by artificial intelligence… scammers are professional manipulators, and nobody is immune.

We offer practical, caring prevention workshops to learn how to recognise traps and respond calmly. Our sessions are for seniors, employees and pupils, in town halls, social services, businesses and schools.

Our method: fables that stick, real and recent cases, role play, and one simple rule to apply every day.

Our belief: the best protection is a simple reflex that everyone understands.
