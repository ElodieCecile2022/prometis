Accessibility

WhitePaw wants its website to be usable by everyone, especially older people and people with disabilities. The site was designed following the French accessibility standard (RGAA 4.1), which incorporates the international WCAG 2.1 level AA criteria.

Compliance status

This website has not yet been audited for RGAA compliance. Without an audit, French regulations require us to state that the site is not compliant, however much care has gone into its design. An audit is planned: this page will be updated with its results.

What we have put in place

  • Large text and colour contrasts meeting level AA.
  • A site that adapts to enlarged text and browser zoom, up to 200%.
  • Full keyboard navigation, with a skip link to the content and a visible focus indicator.
  • Structured headings and page regions identified for screen readers.
  • Illustrations described with alternative text.
  • Forms with clear labels and required fields indicated.
  • Subtitled videos, with a written transcript.
  • Respect for your device's “reduce motion” setting.
  • Downloadable sheets in tagged PDF format, readable by screen readers.
