Hello, I'm Camille. Today: when artificial intelligence imitates the voice of your loved ones.

You get a call, or a voice message, from a loved one in distress, from an unknown number. The voice is perfectly imitated: a few seconds of recording found online are enough. They have an urgent problem, and they ask you for money, in secret.

How do you beat this trap? Hang up, and call your loved one back on their usual number. Ask a question only they know the answer to. Better still: choose a secret family password today, to ask for in an emergency.

That's the lesson of the Wolf and the Kid: show me your white paw!
